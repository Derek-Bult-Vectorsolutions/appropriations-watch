FY2027 Continuing Resolution Active

Congress passed H.R. 6500 (Continuing Appropriations and Extensions Act, 2027) on September 1, 2026, extending government funding at FY2026 levels through December 11, 2026. This pushes appropriations negotiations past the November midterms while committee markup activity continues on all 12 FY2027 bills.
